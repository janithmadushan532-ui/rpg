# Metro Dash – Science MCQ bank notes

The game includes 150 newly authored Sinhala multiple-choice questions: 75 mapped to Grade 10 and 75 mapped to Grade 11. The questions are distributed across the chapter headings listed on the official e-Thaksalawa course pages (20 Grade 10 chapter headings and 15 Grade 11 chapter headings). Each question has four choices, one keyed answer and a short explanation.

Official chapter-list references used for mapping:
- Grade 10 Science: https://e-thaksalawa.moe.gov.lk/lcms/course/view.php?id=844&lang=en
- Grade 11 Science: https://e-thaksalawa.moe.gov.lk/lcms/course/view.php?id=800&lang=en

Questions are original practice questions written to align with the listed topics; they are not copied from official examination papers. Teachers should review the bank against the current school-year teacher guide before formal assessment or publication.

In-game behaviour:
- A question appears every 200 metres.
- The run pauses until an answer is selected and the player continues.
- A correct answer awards 150 score points and 5 coins.
- Question order and progress are saved in browser local storage, so the player can continue the question set over multiple runs.
